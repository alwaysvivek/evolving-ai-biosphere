🌍 Evolving AI Biosphere

Welcome to the technical documentation for the Evolving AI Biosphere. This project is a research-grade simulation exploring Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) and Evolutionary Algorithms in a continuous environment.

🚀 Quick Start

1. ./setup_env.sh (Install dependencies)

2. python3 launch_services.py (Start MLflow & Monitor Ollama)

3. python3 simulation.py (Run the simulation)

1. System Architecture

The system is composed of three decoupled layers: The Simulation Core, The Intelligence Layer, and The Observability Layer.

2. Evolutionary Mechanisms

🧠 The Predator Hive Mind (Deep RL)

Predators do not learn individually; they share a collective brain (a PyTorch LSTM). This allows for rapid adaptation across the entire species.

  • Architecture: LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) network taking 8 inputs (local vision) and outputting 4 continuous probabilities (Move Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • Training: Uses REINFORCE (Policy Gradient). Every 20 generations, a batch of "experiences" (State, Action, Reward) is sampled from the Hive buffer to update the network weights.
  • Reward Function: +1.0 for eating prey, -0.1 for starvation per tick, -5.0 for death.

🧬 Herbivore Genetics (Evolutionary Algorithm)

Herbivores possess individual "genomes" consisting of weights for a simple feed-forward neural network. They evolve via natural selection.

  • Reproduction: Asexual reproduction occurs when energy > threshold.
  • Mutation: Offspring inherit parent weights with a random Gaussian noise (mutation rate = 0.05).
  • Selection Pressure: Only those successful at finding food (plants) survive long enough to reproduce, naturally selecting for efficient foraging behaviors.
  • Photosynthetic Adaptability: In the absence of plants, Herbivores can technically sustain themselves by "resting" in high-light environments, gaining energy directly from the sun (simulating metabolic efficiency/basking).

3. DETAILED BIO-MECHANICS (The "Hidden" Rules)

🌱 Plants

  • Growth Formula: Energy += 3.5 * LocalLight * GlobalLight * TempFactor
  • Reproduction: Requires Energy > 130. Splits energy in half to create offspring.
  • Death: Dies if Energy <= 0 (starvation) or eaten.

🐑 Herbivores

  • Action Space: Move (Cost: 1.0), Rest (Gain: Variable), Eat (Gain: Plant Energy * 0.9).
  • Metabolic Basking: If choosing the "Rest" action, they gain energy: 6.0 * Light * TempFactor. This allows survival even during plant extinction events if conditions are perfect.
  • Reproduction: Requires Energy > Threshold. Asexual cloning with mutation.

🐺 Predators

  • The Hive Mind: All predators share ONE neural network. They do not evolve individually.
  • Hunting Reward: Energy += TargetEnergy * 1.5 (Max 120).
  • Starvation: Losing -1.2 energy per tick. Death at 0 energy (-5.0 Reward penalty to Hive).

4. The "God Mode" Interface

The simulation integrates a local LLM (Llama 3.2 via Ollama) to allow natural language control. Instead of clicking buttons, you can type commands like:

"Spawn a swarm of 20 predators in the top corner"
"Trigger a drought event (reduce plant growth)"
"Kill 50% of the herbivores to balance the population"

The system uses Function Calling to parse these intents and execute the corresponding Simulation API methods.

5. Service Orchestration

To keep the simulation lightweight, heavy services run in the background. The launch_services.py script orchestrates this:

  • MLflow (Port 5001): Logs population stats, energy levels, and extinction events.
  • Ollama (Port 11434): Hosts the Llama 3.2 model for the God Mode agent.
