Theoretical Concepts

Hive Mind Learning (Predators)

Predators do not learn individually. Instead, they share a single LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) policy network. All predator experiences (State, Action, Reward) are collected into a global buffer. Every 20 generations, the Hive Mind performs a REINFORCE policy gradient update. This simulates "collective consciousness" or rapid evolutionary adaptation.

Individual Evolution (Herbivores)

Herbivores possess individual neural networks. When they reproduce, their offspring inherit a copy of the parent's weights with slight random mutations (Gaussian noise). This creates a Darwinian natural selection process where "smarter" weights survive.
