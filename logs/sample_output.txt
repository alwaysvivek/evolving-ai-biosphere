SPACE - Play/Pause
F     - Spawn flower pattern
S     - Spawn spiral galaxy
O     - Spawn predator swarm
N     - Spawn nutrient field
C     - Clear
R     - Print report
T     - Toggle predator training ON/OFF (training runs automatically when ON)
E     - Trigger scarcity event
K     - Kill predators
L     - Kill herbivores
P     - Kill plants
Q     - Quit
